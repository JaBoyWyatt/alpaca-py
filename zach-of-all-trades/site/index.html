    Questions before you book.

    How do I pay?

    Card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, through a secure link. Menu jobs put a card on file and charge when the work is done. No cash or checks.

    What won't you take on?

    New electrical circuits, gas lines, main plumbing and full remodels. Those need a licensed specialist, and I'll point you to a good one.

    How soon can you come out?

    The booking calendar shows real open days. Small jobs often go same week. Photo quotes come back by text, usually within 2 hours during the day.

    Do you bring the parts?

    Bring your own, or I pick them up at cost and hand you the receipt. Screws, anchors, caulk and the small stuff are always included.

    What if the job takes longer than the flat price?

    A flat price is flat. If something hidden changes the job, like rot behind the drywall, you hear about it and approve it before any extra work starts.
