    Common questions

    Do I need to buy the parts?

    For things like a faucet, fan or toilet, you pick the one you like and have it ready. Or I pick it up and charge what I paid, with the receipt. Screws, anchors, caulk and small stuff are always included.

    How do I pay?

    By card, Apple Pay or bank transfer through a secure link, once the job is done. I don't take cash or checks.

    How soon can you come?

    The rate card shows the days I have open. Small jobs often fit in the same week. Photo quotes come back by text, usually within 2 hours during the day.

    What jobs won't you do?

    New electrical circuits, gas lines, main plumbing lines and full remodels. Those need a licensed specialist, and I'll point you to a good one.

    What if it takes longer than expected?

    A flat price stays flat. If I find something hidden, like rot behind drywall, I show you and you decide before anything changes.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Cancel or move your time for free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, or if I drive out and can't get in, the trip fee applies, $35 minimum.
